## 🧠 debate mechanism(Reports that users actually see Tab)

### Trigger condition

Debates are initiated at all depth levels,The difference is**Debate rounds**:1-3 Level Debate 1 round,4 level(Depth)2 wheel,5 level(Comprehensive)3 wheel.

### Division of roles(The tab name that users see)

There are 3 debates related in the report Tab:

| The tab name that users see | backend key | stance | focus |
|------|----|------|--------|
| **🐂 Optimistic Scenario Researcher** | `bull_researcher` | optimistic | looking for**Support the rise**evidence:Growth, breakthrough, positive, valuation restoration |
| **🐻 Prudential Scenario Researcher** | `bear_researcher` | Prudence | looking for**Support falls**evidence:Risks, bubbles, bad news, technical breakdowns |
| **🔬 research integrator** | `research_team_decision` | Neutral | **Integrate both sides**,draw integrated conclusions |

> Note:report**top**and **Tab on**unified display as"🐂 Optimistic Scenario Researcher/🐻 Prudential Scenario Researcher". 

### debate process

1. **🐂 Optimistic Scenario Researcher**Report first(Find all positive evidence)
2. **🐻 Prudential Scenario Researcher**After reading the optimistic report,Submit a rebuttal report(Find evidence of prudence)
3. **🔬 research integrator**After reading two reports,out"Comprehensive integration"Conclusion
4. **risk debate**(⚡ High elasticity / 🛡️ defense / ⚖️ benchmark)3 scenario analysis
5. **👔 risk assessor**Comprehensive tripartite risk assessment
6. **🧾 Comprehensive research conclusion** Integrate all,draw final conclusion

> 💡 **This simulates a real investment committee**:Collision of multiple perspectives,Avoid single point bias.

## 🛡️ 3 risk perspective(Also displayed as Tab name)

After the debate,3 appear in the report**Risk scenario analysis Tab**:

| The tab name that users see | backend key | risk appetite | focus |
|------|----|---------|--------|
| **⚡ Highly Resilient Scenario Analyst** | `risky_analyst` | Enterprising | **Opportunities outweigh risks**,enterprising perspective |
| **🛡️ Defense Scenario Analyst** | `safe_analyst` | conservative | **Risks outweigh opportunities**,conservative perspective |
| **⚖️ Baseline Scenario Analyst** | `neutral_analyst` | Neutral | **Risk opportunity balance**,objective perspective |

eventually **👔 risk assessor**(`risk_management_decision`)Comprehensive tripartite risk assessment,out ⚠️ Risk Review Tab.

## 🖱️ How to read a debate report

### steps 1:Submit analysis(Want to see the fullest debate,Choose level 4-5)

1. Click the "Single Stock Research" menu on the sidebar
2. Choose depth(Want to see the fullest debate,choose **4 level(Depth)** or **5 level(Comprehensive)**)
3. When the analyst checks,Choose 2-4 according to the scene(The remaining research roles are automatically generated by the workflow.)
4. Submit analysis

### steps 2:View report Tab order

After the report is completed,in**Report details page**See full tab list.**Focus on this order**:

```
1. 📈 market analyst / 💰 fundamental analyst        ← objective data
2. 🐂 Optimistic Scenario Researcher                      ← optimistic view
3. 🐻 Prudential Scenario Researcher                      ← Prudent view
4. 🔬 research integrator                          ← Comprehensive
5. ⚡ High elasticity / 🛡️ defense / ⚖️ Benchmark Risk Three Perspectives  ← risk
6. 👔 risk assessor                          ← risk synthesis
7. 🧾 Research conclusion                        ← final conclusion
```

### steps 3:identify"decision making"key signals

When reading the debate report,Follow**these signals**:

| signal | meaning |
|------|------|
| 🐂 report**weak reason**(Just talk about concepts) | Optimism and lack of confidence |
| 🐻 report**weak reason** | Lack of prudent confidence |
| two researchers**The conclusions are quite different** | this stock**controversial**,high risk |
| two researchers**The conclusion is consistent** | Strong consensus,Can refer to |
| 🔬 research integrator**Many doubts** | The system is in"Hedging",🧾 The comprehensive research conclusion may be"Neutral" |
| 3 risk analyst**All conservative** | The system considers the risks to be greater than the opportunities |

## 📊 Practical cases(Based on real report samples)

**stocks**: Ningde era 300750
**Analysis date**: 2026-01-24
**Depth**: 5 level(Comprehensive)

### 🐂 Optimistic Scenario Researchers Core Conclusions

> "The prosperity of the new energy industry continues,The company's global market share increased,Lithium battery technology leadership,2025 Q4 shipments exceeded expectations,Both revenue and profit growth rates are higher than the industry average."

### 🐻 Prudential Scenario Researcher Core Conclusions

> "The short-term increase is too fast,RSI Entering the overbought zone,Industry competition intensifies(BYD Blade Battery, Solid State Battery Impact),Valuations reflect optimistic expectations,There is a risk of a pullback."

### 🔬 Comprehensive conclusions from the research integrator

> "Both sides make sense:Optimists focus on mid- to long-term prosperity and performance fulfillment,Prudence points to the view that the short-term gains are too large and the valuation has already factored in more optimistic expectations. At present, the evidence of both parties has not been fully verified.,More likely to fluctuate in the short term,The mid- to long-term depends on performance realization."

### 🧾 Comprehensive research conclusion

- **Decision label**:**Neutral**(Systematic research conclusion tendencies,non-trading orders)
- **🧾 Comprehensive research conclusion** Tab content:One sentence conclusion——"The current study is judged to be neutral.,Both optimism and caution are under-tested.";core basis——"Mid- to long-term prosperity and performance are supported,However, short-term gains and valuations have been factored into more optimistic expectations."
- **⚠️ risk review** Tab:
  - risk level:medium
  - risk score:0.42
  - Assessment conclusion:The core contradiction is the information vacuum in the annual report rather than the imbalance of direction.

> **Interpretation**:Although there are grounds for both optimism and caution,But**Optimistic evidence(Mid- to long-term prosperity)and prudent party evidence(Short-term valuations are on the high side)have not yet been fully verified**,Balance of reasons for both sides,So the decision label is"Neutral". 
>
> ⚠️ **important**:Decision label"Optimistic/cautious/neutral"It is a systematic tendency of research conclusions,**Not a trading order**. "Neutral"not equal to"hold"or"wait and see",The final buying and selling decision is yours to make.

## ❓ FAQ

**Q:Will the debate mechanism slow down analysis??**
A: yes,4 Level 4-5 minutes slower than Level 3,5 Level 5-10 minutes slower than Level 4. But**before important decisions**It's worth spending a few extra minutes.

**Q:🐂 optimistic and 🐻 Prudence The opposite conclusion,Who should I believe?**
A: **Believe it all**. This is the value of debate——Avoid single point bias. Focus on **🔬 research integrator** How to synthesize,and **🧾 Comprehensive research conclusion** How to make a decision.

**Q:If the debate final decision label is"optimistic",Is it time to buy??**
A: No. The system output is**Research reference**,Not a trading order. Decision label"Optimistic/cautious/neutral"Only expresses the system's research conclusion tendency for this stock,**not equal to"Buy/Sell/Hold"**. Make a comprehensive judgment based on your own risk preference, capital situation, and market environment.

**Q:3 What is the use of risk perspective??**
A: help you understand"How would people with different risk preferences view this stock?". If you are conservative,But all risk analysts are aggressive,may indicate**This stock is indeed low risk**. 

**Q:Can we just read the debate report?,Don't look at technical/fundamental aspects?**
A: Not recommended. The debate is**Deduction based on basic data**,no basic data,debate"air to air". 

**Q:What is the difference between depth level 4 and level 5 debates??**
A: The characters running in both levels are the same.(🐂 optimistic / 🐻 Prudence + 🔬 Integrate + ⚡ / 🛡️ / ⚖️ Three risk perspectives),The difference is**Debate rounds**:4 Level Debate Round 2,5 Level Debate 3 rounds. Level 5 is more researched,But**Conclusions usually don't differ much**.
