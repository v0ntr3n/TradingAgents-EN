## 🎯 Project Positioning

TradingAgents-CN is a research assistance system tailored for the Chinese A-share market. Multiple AI agents playing distinct roles (macro / sector / market / fundamental / news analysts, bullish / bearish researchers, etc.) collaborate to complete the research workflow, helping you systematically learn multi-agent frameworks and the application of large language models in equity research.

Starting as a multi-agent analysis tool in v1.0, the project evolved through a unified analysis engine and visual workflows in v2.0, research-assistance compliance alignment in v2.1, and into a unified AI assistant entry point in v3.0.

> This project is a **research assistance tool**. All AI-generated content is for learning and reference only and **does not constitute investment advice**.
